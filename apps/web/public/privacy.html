Privacy Policy

Scripture Guide Chrome extension. Last updated November 20, 2026.

The Scripture Guide Chrome extension finds scripture references on the web pages you visit, such as "John 3:16" or "Alma 32:21", and turns them into links to that passage on scripture.guide. It does not collect, sell or share personal data.

What the extension reads

To find references, the extension reads the text of the pages you visit. That text is processed inside the extension, on your own computer. It is never sent to scripture.guide or to any other server, and it is not stored.

When you open the extension's toolbar popup, it reads the address of the current tab, only to show whether the extension is turned on for that site.

What the extension stores

  • Your settings, such as the sites it is turned on or off for, scripture language and canon, detection options and link style. These are saved with Chrome's own storage, and Chrome syncs them across your signed-in devices if you have Chrome sync turned on.
  • A short list of sites where scanning was paused to keep the page responsive, so those sites start with lighter scanning for the next 7 days. This list stays on your computer.

Nothing the extension stores is sent to us.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not collect personal information, browsing history, page content or activity.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • It does not load code from outside the extension.
  • It does not sell or transfer any data to anyone.

When you open a link

Clicking a link the extension added, using the right-click "Look up in Scripture Guide" item, or typing sg in the address bar opens a page on scripture.guide, like following any other link. As with any website, scripture.guide's server receives that request.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be raised on the project's GitHub issues page.
