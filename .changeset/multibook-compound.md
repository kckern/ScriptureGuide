A range across books after another piece is no longer dropped: `lookupReference("John 3:16; Malachi 4:5-Matthew 1:2")` returns both pieces (it returned only "John 3:16"). Such a range does not change the running book, as on scripture.guide: in "John 3:16; Malachi 4:5-Matthew 1:2; 3" the bare "3" is John 3.
