"scripture-guide-chrome-extension": patch
---

A link on the page whose whole text is a scripture reference (for example jw.org's "Acts 4:12") now becomes a Scripture Guide badge, with a small chain-link icon after it that keeps the site's original link. Before, these were only marked by a faint icon and looked as if they had not been detected. Links with other words in them keep the icon. Turning the extension off for a site restores the original links.
