This page contains various scripture references to test the extension:

  • John 3:16 - Popular Bible verse

  • D&C 88:63 - Doctrine and Covenants
  • Moses 1:39 - Pearl of Great Price

Paragraph Text

In 1 Nephi 3:7, Nephi expresses his faith by saying he will go and do what the Lord commands. This connects beautifully with John 14:15 where Jesus teaches about keeping His commandments. The Savior also taught in Matthew 7:7-8 about asking, seeking, and knocking.

Modern revelation in D&C 130:20-21 teaches about blessings being predicated upon obedience to law. This principle is also found in Mosiah 2:41 and Alma 41:10.
